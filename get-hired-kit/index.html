Why nobody replies

It is not your skills.
It is your first two lines.

A VA job post often gets 50 or more applications. The person hiring reads the first two lines of each one, on their phone, between other things.

Most applications open with "I am writing to apply" and a list of skills. Those first two lines could have been sent to any job, so they get skipped.

The ones that get a reply name the main task, the tools and the time zone from the post, before anything else. That proves you read it. This kit makes you do that on every application, in about 10 minutes.
